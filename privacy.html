Privacy Policy — Peek Doctor Ultimate

Last updated: 2026-05-16

Peek Doctor Ultimate is a Chrome extension that lets you preview where a link leads without leaving the page you are on — hover a link, tap a modifier key, and a live, scrollable, draggable, resizable copy of the destination drops in over the page. This page explains exactly what data the extension touches, what it does with it, and what it does not do.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, URLs, page content, analytics, telemetry, or usage data. There are no accounts, no logins, and no remote servers operated by us.

What the extension reads locally

The extension reads only its own previously-saved preferences (your chosen trigger key, hold delay, and remembered preview-window size and position). It observes mouse position and keyboard modifier state on pages you visit in order to detect a hover-and-modifier gesture on a link — this happens entirely inside your browser and is never sent anywhere.

What the extension stores

The extension uses Chrome's local storage API on your own device to remember:

  • your selected trigger modifier (e.g. Shift / Alt / Ctrl)
  • your selected hold delay (instant or hold-to-open)
  • the last size and position of the preview window

This data stays on your machine. It is never uploaded.

Permissions, and why we need them

The extension requests storage to save the preferences above, declarativeNetRequest to allow the destination page to render inside the in-page preview overlay (by stripping framing-related response headers on the preview request only), and host access to all sites so the hover-preview gesture can work on any page you visit. No request bodies, URLs, or page contents are read, logged, or transmitted by the extension.

Third parties

The extension does not embed analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs, and does not load remote code. Previews load destination URLs directly from your browser to those destinations, exactly as if you had opened them yourself.

Children

The extension is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy
